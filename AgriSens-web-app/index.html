Government Schemes & Farmer Support

Access trusted government schemes, subsidies, insurance, and direct farming support in one place for informed decisions and faster help.

PM-Kisan Scheme

Direct income support for farmers, helping secure cash flow for crop expenses and family needs.

  • Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers with registered land.
  • Benefits: ₹6,000 per year in three instalments.
Learn More Apply Now

Crop Insurance

Protect crops from weather shocks, pest loss, and other risks with affordable government-backed insurance.

  • Eligibility: Farmers with notified crops and insured land.
  • Benefits: Compensation for crop damage and loss.
Learn More Apply Now

Fertilizer Subsidy

Lower the cost of essential fertilizer and improve nutrient use while supporting sustainable crop growth.

  • Eligibility: Registered farmers buying subsidized fertilizer.
  • Benefits: Reduced input cost for balanced nutrition.
Learn More Apply Now

Farming Loan Support

Get low-interest credit for seeds, equipment, and working capital through government farm loan schemes.

  • Eligibility: Farmers with valid KYC and land records.
  • Benefits: Affordable credit for smooth farming operations.
Learn More Apply Now

Tractor/Machine Subsidy

Support for buying machinery, pumps, and equipment to reduce manual effort and increase farm efficiency.

  • Eligibility: Farmers, SHGs, and custom hiring centres.
  • Benefits: Subsidy on tractors, harvesters, and irrigation pumps.
Learn More Apply Now

Irrigation Support

Access subsidies for drip, sprinkler and micro-irrigation systems to use water more efficiently.

  • Eligibility: Farmers with irrigation requirements and land records.
  • Benefits: Lower water use, higher yield, and better crop security.
Learn More Apply Now

Need Support?

For help with scheme registration, application steps, or general support, use the farmer helpline and official resources below.

How to Use

Choose the scheme that best matches your needs, review eligibility, and follow the links to official government portals for reliable, up-to-date information.

  • See eligibility and benefits at a glance.

  • Keep documents ready: ID, land papers, and bank details.
